A 3D crime story from behind the detective.

The camera follows you through tight stairwells, dim hallways, and cramped apartments. The case takes shape as you find what people left behind and bring it into the interrogation room.

  1. Arrive at the crime scene

    Walk the scene yourself, from the taped-off entrance to the service stairs behind the building.

  2. Interrogate suspects

    Sit across from the suspects. Put a piece of evidence on the table and see which details in their stories change.

  3. Find the culprit

    Trace the route through the building, compare alibis, and work out who could have opened the locked door.

The crime scene

Look a little closer.

The police tape is already up when you reach the Calder building. The victim was found in a locked apartment. The street is soaked, but the stairwell tells a different story.

Beneath the radiator

A brass key lies under the dust. Someone has filed the apartment number off its tag.

Inside the porter’s ledger

The line for 11:43 p.m. has been torn out. The page underneath still carries the impression of a name.

On the service stairs

A dry footprint breaks a trail of wet ones. Someone may have been inside before the rain started.

The suspects

Three people. Three stories.

Each was near the Calder building that night. Each remembers something the others left out.

Night porter

Elias Ward

He insists the service entrance stayed locked all night. The missing ledger entry was on his shift.

Upstairs tenant

Mara Voss

She heard two voices in the stairwell. She can describe the argument word for word, but claims she recognized neither voice.

Locksmith

Jonah Vale

He changed the lock that afternoon. He says he handed every copy of the key to the victim.

Interrogation excerpt

Put the ledger on the table.

Elias Ward sits across from you under a bare bulb. You turn the porter’s ledger toward him and point to the torn page.

  1. You

    “You said nobody used the service entrance. Whose name was on the line you tore out?”

  2. Elias Ward

    “I found it like that when I came back.”

  3. You

    “Came back from where?”

  4. Elias Ward

    “The service stairs. I heard someone at the door.”
